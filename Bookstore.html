Ikigai

Author: Hector Garcia

Ikigai invites you to explore the timeless idea of ikigai, a Japanese concept meaning "a reason to live" or "a reason to get up in the morning." Blending ancient wisdom with contemporary research, it guides readers toward the sweet spot where passion, mission, vocation, and profession intersect. Written for curious adults and anyone seeking a more meaningful daily life, this book offers a calm, hopeful, and educational tone that invites reflection and action. Structured as a thoughtful guide, Ikigai weaves short chapters with reflective exercises you can pause on, revisit, and apply. It shares inspiring stories from Okinawa to illustrate how purpose shapes everyday life, and the pace is steady and approachable so readers can absorb ideas without feeling overwhelmed.
