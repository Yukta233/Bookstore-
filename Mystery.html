As Good As Dead

Author: Holly Jackson

As Good As Dead is the thrilling conclusion to the bestselling A Good Girl's Guide to Murder series. In this high-stakes YA mystery thriller, Pip Fitz-Amobi heads to college under the weight of online threats and a chilling message: "Who will look for you when you're the one who disappears?" Perfect for teen and adult readers who crave clever plotting, gutsy protagonists, and edge-of-your-seat suspense, this final installment delivers a tense, emotional ride that lingers long after the last page. Written in a lean, immersive voice, As Good As Dead unfolds through Pip's determined perspective as she navigates a new campus, old secrets, and a growing web of danger. The book is structured to tighten the suspense with brisk chapters, sharp deductions, and moments of quiet reflection that reveal Pip's resilience. The experience is immersive and cinematic—pulsing with twists, evocative settings, and a relentless pursuit of truth that makes the reader feel every step Pip takes.

Author: Holly Jackson

The Reappearance Of Rachel Price is an exhilarating young adult thriller that pulls you into a mystery of disappearance and reunion. Five Survive

Author: Holly Jackson

Five Survive follows eighteen-year-old Red and her friends as their Spring Break road trip turns from sunny escape to dangerous ground. This standalone YA crime thriller blends adrenaline-fueled suspense with sharp, honest explorations of friendship under pressure, crafted for readers who crave mystery, danger, and coming-of-age grit. The tone is taut, brave, and emotionally aware—an edge-of-your-seat ride that matters as much as the night is long. Written with lean, propulsive prose, Five Survive tightens the screws as an RV breaks down in a desolate stretch of road and a sniper closes in. The narrative unfolds as a relentless game of pursuit and secrets, pairing heart-pounding chase scenes with quiet, revealing moments that probe trust, loyalty, and what people will do to survive. If you enjoy character-driven thrillers, you’ll be drawn into Red’s perspective and the brisk pacing that keeps you turning pages without spoiling the twists.

Then She Was Gone

Author: Lisa Jewell

Then She Was Gone is a gripping psychological thriller about a mother’s decade-long search for truth after her daughter vanishes. This darkly emotional mystery invites adult readers who love suspense with heart, blending grief, resilience, and a quest for answers into a page-turning experience. Written with a deft, warm touch, Then She Was Gone follows Laurel Mack as she tries to piece her life back together after Ellie’s disappearance. A chance encounter in a café pulls Laurel into a new connection that unsettles the quiet of her days and stirs long-buried questions. The narrative moves between the present and the years that followed Ellie’s vanishing, weaving memories with new discoveries at a steady, immersive pace. The book feels intimate, with vivid settings, believable characters, and a storytelling rhythm that keeps you turning pages as you weigh what trust really means when the truth might still be out there.

Verity

Author: Collen Hoover

Verity is a pulse-pounding psychological thriller that follows Lowen Ashleigh, a struggling writer who is hired to finish the novels of bestselling author Verity Crawford. This gripping tale, suited for adult readers who crave twisty, morally complex stories, blends fear, desire, and deception into a fast-paced, immersive experience. The paperback edition invites you to step into a world where every choice has consequences and nothing is quite what it seems. Structured with lean, cinematic momentum, the story unfolds across two intertwined perspectives: Lowen's present-day investigation and a hidden autobiography discovered in the chaos of the author's office. The manuscript reveals dark secrets Verity never intended to share, turning each revelation into a test of trust and restraint. As Lowen weighs the risks of exposing the truth to Verity's grieving husband, the tension tightens and the moral stakes rise, pushing the boundaries between loyalty, love, and obligation. Hoover's prose is sharp, cinematic, and propulsive, delivering quick, gripping chapters and a pace that urges you to turn the page.
